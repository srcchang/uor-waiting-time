# Privacy Policy

UOR Waiting Time ("the app", "we", "us") is an independent app that shows wait times and park information for Universal Orlando Resort. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have.

**Contact:** srcchang@gmail.com

## 1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, account, or precise location. The app creates a randomly generated anonymous identifier through Firebase Authentication so that app services can work without a personal account. This identifier is not your name or email address.

The app and its service providers may process:

- Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, app version, language, and general region
- App usage events, such as app start and ad impressions
- Crash reports and diagnostic logs
- A coarse region derived from network information, not GPS
- Advertising identifiers, subject to your consent and platform settings

## 2. How we use information

- Provide live wait times, park hours, closures, attraction details, and trends
- Keep the app stable and fix crashes
- Understand aggregate usage and improve features
- Show ads that keep the app free
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. Firebase and Google services

The app uses Firebase (Google LLC) for anonymous authentication, Cloud Firestore (content and wait-time data), Cloud Storage (images and content files), Remote Config (feature flags and update thresholds), Analytics (aggregate usage events), and Crashlytics (crash reports). Google's privacy policy applies to its processing.

## 4. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your consent and device settings. On iOS, the app may ask for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. You can change ad personalization choices in the app under Settings → Privacy options, and reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings. We do not sell personal information.

## 5. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on legitimate interests to operate and improve the app, consent for personalized advertising and tracking, and legal obligations where applicable. You can withdraw consent at any time through the app or your device settings.

## 6. Retention

- Live wait-time snapshots and aggregated statistics are retained to provide trends and operate the service.
- Crash and diagnostic data are retained by Firebase Crashlytics according to Google's retention settings, typically up to 90 days.
- Analytics events are retained according to Firebase Analytics retention settings, up to 14 months.
- The anonymous identifier is retained while needed to operate the app. You can reset it by clearing app data or reinstalling the app.

## 7. Sharing

We share information with service providers that help operate the app, including Google Firebase, Google AdMob, and the app stores. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety. We do not sell personal information.

## 8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to data portability. Because the app does not use personal accounts, we may not be able to identify your specific records. Contact us at srcchang@gmail.com and we will respond as required by law.

You can also:

- Turn off personalized ads in the app or in your device settings
- Limit ad tracking on iOS
- Clear app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored preferences
- Contact us to request deletion of crash or analytics data associated with your app instance

## 9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us.

## 10. International transfers

Service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards.

## 11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections over HTTPS and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## 12. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to serve the site and protect its service. The website does not use analytics cookies or advertising trackers. See GitHub's privacy statement.

## 13. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

## 14. Contact

Questions or requests: srcchang@gmail.com.
